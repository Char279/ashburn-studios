The studio

Ashburn Studios

Ashburn Studios is an independent game studio. Compost Comrades is our first release: a small, dense incremental game with hand-drawn pixel art, built in Unity and headed to Steam for Windows.

We like games you can play with one hand, quotas that feel just slightly unfair, and vegetables with faces.
